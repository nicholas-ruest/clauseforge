# ClauseForge product specification

ClauseForge gives procurement counsel and AI-governance leads a controlled way to evaluate supplier-AI contract amendments before approval. A reviewer identifies the supplier, proposed clause change, affected-control scope, and contractual cure window. The system authenticates and authorizes the review, validates amendment facts, executes ten Rust domain stages plus eight source-pinned upstream responsibilities, computes a transparent residual-risk score, and seals the obligation/control/mitigation decision into a restart-safe audit chain.

## Acceptance outcomes

1. An authorized amendment reviewer receives a completed result with eighteen named stages, correlation ID, residual-risk score, and evidence seal.
2. An observer or over-boundary control request is denied before any upstream call or durable write; restoring reviewer authority completes the same authored amendment.
3. A saved supplier-amendment decision remains visible after browser refresh and backend restart with the same correlation and digest.

The product is local-first and deployable by configuration only. It does not provision cloud resources, trigger production failures, merge its own PR, or claim that its prototype score is a probability. Source integrity, complete evidence and human review are release constraints rather than optional hardening.
