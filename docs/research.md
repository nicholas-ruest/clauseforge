# Research and selection synthesis

The evaluated frontier is evidence-bearing operational resilience: can an amendment review combine semantic similarity, calibrated inference, stream safety, post-quantum envelopes and federated receipts while retaining enterprise policy and message delivery boundaries? Twelve Ruvnet and six enterprise candidates were evaluated. Selection chose six distinct Ruvnet projects—RuVector, ruv-FANN, midstream, QuDAG, guardrail and dspy.ts—and two enterprise projects—OPA and LogAct.

Every selected exact revision passed an intended operation and a substantive failure path before implementation. The selection gate used the probe harness source SHA `9487e020260272fb28c0833e36c575c0cafe3871`; raw logs and receipts remain external. Metadata, imports and help output were not credited.

The benchmark hypothesis is that the eight-capability path improves accountable stage coverage and audit detail at a measurable latency cost. The ablation removes upstream stages without changing the request. ClauseForge records latency, stage count and score delta; it does not generalize these prototype results into production reliability claims.
